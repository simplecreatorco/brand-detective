Step 1 of 3
Your Buyer
This section is about them, not you. Who are you talking to and what's their struggle?
💡 These answers power your Brand Voice Guide AND your website copy. The more you write, the more the AI has to work with, and the more your copy will actually sound like you. Don't give one-liners here.
🎤
Hate typing? Click the mic icon on any text box to talk instead. You'll get way more detail that way.
Who are you talking to?
Describe the person landing on your site. What's her day like? What is she doing when she finds you? What's going through her head? Get specific.
What specific problem are you solving?
Not "helping them succeed." What actual, tangible pain point? Describe it the way she would describe it to a friend.
What's the villain in their story?
Not a person. What's the real thing working against them? Bad advice, overwhelm, the algorithm, lack of time, imposter syndrome, confusing tech? Name the enemy they're fighting.
How does all of this make them feel?
People buy based on how a problem makes them feel, not just the problem itself. Frustrated? Embarrassed? Like a fraud? Behind? Overwhelmed? Describe the emotional weight they carry.
What does life look like after they work with you?
Paint the full picture. What's different in their day-to-day? How do they feel? What are they doing that they weren't doing before?
Why should they trust you over everyone else?
Your experience, your results, your approach. What's your story that proves you're the real deal? Don't be humble here. Tell it like it is.
What happens if they do nothing?
What stays the same? What do they miss out on? Describe the cost of inaction. Not to scare them, but to help them see what's at stake.
